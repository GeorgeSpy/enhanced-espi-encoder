## Claim Boundary Note

The official reportable v6.2 metrics come from the ResNet18-style v6.2-A baseline. The full hierarchical / physics v6.2 architecture is presented as a research basis for future encoder work and is not identical to the reportable validation result.

## Model Levels

| Level | Short description | Role |
|---|---|---|
| v6.1 | Frozen ResNet-18 backbone with a trainable 5-class head. | Simple and stable modal-classification baseline. |
| v6.2-A reportable baseline | ResNet18-style baseline with distribution/noise-aware training strategy. | Official baseline result and apples-to-apples comparison against v6.1. |
| Hierarchical / physics v6.2 | Physics/Fourier front-end, gatekeeper, expert branch, and ArcFace-style metric component. | Research basis for the future Physics-Aligned Encoder. |
| Enhanced ESPI pipeline | Denoising, Fourier prior, phase unwrapping, physics features, and uncertainty-aware classification. | Infrastructure for physically interpretable ESPI representations. |

## Next Development Path

| Step | Technical action | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Encoder extraction | Isolate the feature/embedding section from the current pipeline. | ESPI embedding per sample. |

| Image-only baseline | Predict acoustic targets from ESPI embeddings only. | Test of standalone ESPI predictive value. |
