### Core Metrology & Representation Novelty

Electronic speckle pattern interferometry (ESPI) is a cornerstone of full-field optical metrology, providing high-resolution measurements of vibration mode shapes. While deep learning models have achieved remarkable success in classifying these interferometric patterns, standard stratified evaluations often overestimate a model’s actual robustness. A model can easily exploit local specular textures or frequency-correlated shortcuts rather than learning a reusable, physically meaningful representation of the underlying modal deformation.

This study addresses this fundamental gap by establishing a **rigorous representation-level audit protocol for frozen ESPI encoders** under strict specimen-level distribution shifts. Our work evaluates generic visual backbones, ESPI-specific deep classifiers (v6.1 and v6.2-A), and a custom physics-aware hierarchical CNN under newly defined **Board-grouped (Leave-One-Board-Out, LOBO)** and **Material-grouped (Leave-One-Material-Out, LOMO)** validation designs.

The key contributions and findings presented in this manuscript include:
1. **Specimen-Invariant Feature Spaces:** We demonstrate that our domain-mixed, distribution-aware encoder (**v6.2-A**) learns an embedding space organized primarily by modal shape rather than specimen geometry. Under strict Board LOBO, v6.2-A emerges as the strongest image-derived frozen encoder baseline, achieving **93.56% Board LOBO Macro-F1** and **93.49% Material LOMO Macro-F1**, significantly outperforming the standard v6.1 baseline which drops to 87.10% and 86.33% respectively.
2. **Suppression of Metrological Confusions:** Per-class diagnostics reveal that the v6.2-A representation heavily suppresses cross-class confusions between structurally similar minority modes (e.g., the confusion between complex torsional mode `2_1` and two-axial bending mode `1_2` is reduced from 13.7% in v6.1 to only **2.2%** in v6.2-A).
3. **Information-Budget Metadata Controls:** We introduce a strict frequency-only baseline control, establishing that the five-class modal protocol is heavily frequency-structured (excitation frequency alone yields 97.49% Board LOBO Macro-F1). This critical finding sets a rigorous information-budget boundary, showing that image-derived performance must be evaluated alongside excitation frequency to prevent overclaiming.
4. **Architectural vs. Objective Trade-offs:** We audit a technically valid 17-layer physics-aware hierarchical CNN containing denoising, Fourier-prior, and phase-unwrapping layers. While structurally elegant, its frozen embedding performance (~26% Board LOBO) demonstrates that architectural priors alone do not guarantee robust post-hoc embedding geometry without representation-aligned training objectives.
